## Why IRANSans is not included

The reviewed [`akiarostami/iransans`](https://github.com/akiarostami/iransans) mirror does not grant font redistribution rights. Its README says users must obtain IRANSans rights from FontIran, it contains no font license, and its font metadata states “All Rights Reserved.” The package's MIT label covers its source/CSS packaging, not the commercial font binaries.

Persian Font Switcher therefore contains no IRANSans binary, preview, or manifest entry. A public Git repository is not evidence of permission to redistribute a commercial font.
